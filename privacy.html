Privacy Policy

1. Client-Side Data Processing

ScholarKit provides research utilities designed to operate locally within your web browser. Uploaded files, citations, bibliographies, and dataset contents are processed using client-side JavaScript and are never transmitted, saved, or stored on external servers.

2. Cookies and Analytics

We may use cookies and standard web analytics to evaluate traffic volume, monitor errors, and improve tool performance. These services collect non-personally identifiable technical details such as browser version and operating system.

Third-party advertising partners, including Google AdSense, may serve ads on this website. These vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this or other websites.
