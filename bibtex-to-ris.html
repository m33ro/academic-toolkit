How BibTeX to RIS Conversion Works

While BibTeX is standard across LaTeX workflows, reference managers like EndNote, Covidence, and Rayyan require Research Information Systems (RIS) formatting. This utility maps standard BibTeX entry types directly to RIS tags without sending your data to any external server.
